Tape reel test: sunny days and dark memories

Four home-video scenes through the 1994 camcorder treatment. Pick one, or press Reel to cut between them like a mixed tape. Space or a click on the video pauses and unpauses.

Sunny tapes are what units haven't done in years: sun, sand, grass, sky. Lots of motion (waves, kite, clouds), so they're easy to read early on. They're the "memories you're allowed before rest".
Dark tapes are low light, so they're noisy and have little motion. That makes them naturally harder to read, without any trick. Good for later levels.
Hidden hint: the sunny tapes are dated in the 1990s, the dark ones in the 2040s. Same camcorder look, but dated after the world turned. Nothing says why. The player may or may not notice.
Reel mode shows how mixing tones could work: mostly sunny, with darker tapes creeping in as the levels go on, the way the lore creeps in.
